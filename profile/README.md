# Icaro Foundation

Icaro Lab is the research laboratory of Icaro Foundation, an Italian nonprofit legally registered as **Fondazione Icaro ETS**. We study the behaviour and safety of AI models and agents and develop methods for their evaluation.

Our public tools and datasets include:

- [MASE](https://github.com/icaro-lab/MASE) — infrastructure for building, running and inspecting controlled multi-agent simulations.
- [Adversarial Humanities Benchmark](https://github.com/icaro-lab/ahb) — a benchmark for testing the robustness of model refusals under humanities-inspired adversarial reformulations.

The Foundation is registered in Italy's National Register of the Third Sector (RUNTS), entry **168258**, since **17 April 2026**. See our [legal and governance details](https://www.icarofoundation.org/transparency/) and [evaluation independence policy](https://www.icarofoundation.org/independence/).
